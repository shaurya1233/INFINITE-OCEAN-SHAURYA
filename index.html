A BRIEF FIELD GUIDE

Take your time with the sea.

INFINITE OCEAN is a conceptual, procedural 3D maritime visualization. Move between a cinematic view, a free orbit camera and a walkable deck connected to the vessel's interior.

Explore on desktop

Choose Exterior or Interior, click the scene to capture the mouse, then use WASD to move. Move the mouse to look. Press Escape to release it.

Explore on mobile

Drag across the scene to look around. Use the directional pad to move. Tap a room label to visit that space.

Interact

Approach highlighted objects and press E or the on-screen prompt for additional details. Walk through the aft doorway to transition between deck and interior. The cabin bed supports a resting interaction. Visit the bridge and choose Take the Helm to drive using gears 1–5, R and N.

What is simulated?

The waves use procedural vertex displacement and shader lighting; the driver uses a speed-dependent arcade marine model, not full hydrodynamics. Reflections and foam are approximations. The framed portrait loads the real ./shaurya.jpg asset when supplied beside this HTML file.
